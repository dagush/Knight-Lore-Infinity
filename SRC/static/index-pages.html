Knight Lore Infinity

This is an experimental, non-profit, just-for-fun extension of the original 1984 Knight Lore, running inside JSSpeccy. Load your own legally obtained .z80 snapshot with the emulator's Open control to begin.

Advance conversations and choose replies with the number keys 0-9. Press B to shoot a bouncing ball, but take care: your own weapon can hurt you too.

The original finite castle has become a deterministic infinite map, with dispersed charms, cauldrons, guarded rooms, original-room interiors, persistent exploration, and an external logical navigation map. This remains a research toy rather than a polished remake, so unintended glitches may still occur.

Source code, technical notes, and project history: dagush/Knight-Lore-Infinity.
